## Daily Check-In

Before each workout, complete this simple assessment to track your readiness.

### Questionnaire (Rate 1-10)

| Question | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Overall, how do you FEEL? (1=terrible, 10=best ever) | __/10 |
| How has your sleep been? | __/10 |
| How motivated are you for training? | __/10 |
| How much are you enjoying training/playing? | __/10 |
| How does your body feel? (soreness, injuries) | __/10 |
| **Total** | **__/50** |

### Countermovement Jump (CMJ) Test

Measure your vertical jump before warming up to track daily readiness.

**Apps for measurement:**
- [MyJump - iPhone](https://apps.apple.com)
- [MyJump - Google Play](https://play.google.com)

### What This Means

Track these numbers day-to-day! If your countermovement jump is significantly lower than usual and/or the questionnaire results come back lower than usual, take it light today to recover.
